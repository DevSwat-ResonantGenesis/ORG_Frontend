## Sales Messaging Framework

### Elevator Pitch (30 seconds)
"ResonantGenesis is the only AI governance platform that enforces policies BEFORE execution, not after. We give every AI agent a cryptographic identity, graduated autonomy based on earned trust, and blockchain-verified audit trails. Enterprises can deploy AI with confidence - governed, auditable, and secure."

### Value Proposition (2 minutes)
"Traditional AI platforms monitor what agents do after the fact. That's too late for regulated industries. ResonantGenesis enforces governance policies BEFORE execution - actions can be approved, flagged, or blocked based on domain-specific rules.

Every user and agent has a cryptographic identity on Ethereum, so there's no anonymous execution. Agent capabilities unlock gradually through our T0-T4 trust tier system - autonomy is earned, not granted. And every action is recorded in an immutable ledger with blockchain-verified timestamps.

For developers, we provide a complete toolkit: Python and JavaScript SDKs, 53 production-ready UI components, 13 agent templates, and comprehensive documentation. What takes months to build from scratch, you can deploy in days with ResonantGenesis."
