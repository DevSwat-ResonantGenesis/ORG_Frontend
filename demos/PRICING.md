## Pricing Psychology

### Why These Numbers

- **$29** — Under the "approval threshold" for most devs
- **$99/seat** — Standard B2B SaaS pricing
- **Min 3 seats** — Ensures team adoption, not lone wolf
- **Enterprise custom** — Don't leave money on the table

### What We're NOT Selling

- ❌ "AI features"
- ❌ "Code generation"
- ❌ "Productivity boost"
- ❌ "Platform access"

### What We ARE Selling

- ✓ **Trust** — "AI that proves its work"
- ✓ **Safety** — "Automatic rollback"
- ✓ **Accountability** — "Complete audit trail"
- ✓ **Control** — "Cost caps you set"

## Objection Handling

**"Why pay when Copilot is cheaper?"**
> "Copilot suggests. You verify. You fix when it breaks.
> We execute, verify automatically, and prove the result.
> How much is your time worth?"

**"Can I just use the free tier forever?"**
> "Yes. 50 tasks/month covers light usage.
> If you're doing serious work, you'll hit the limit in a week."

**"Why per-seat for teams?"**
> "Every team member gets their own audit trail.
> You can see who used AI, what they did, what it cost.
> That's team accountability."

**"What if AI costs exceed the included amount?"**
> "You get a warning before execution.
> You can approve the extra cost or cancel.
> No surprise bills."
